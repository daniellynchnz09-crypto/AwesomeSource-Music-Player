**CONTEXT:**

I have previously been using Soundcloud to source my music but it started giving me some bad ads that I couldn't stand so I decided to go for my phone's inbuilt music player instead. But that music player is a bit of a mess and has problems distinguising the albums that are on my phone and also doesn't have a lot of the cool features that Soundcloud has. So I have decided that I am going to vibe code my own music player so I can get the best of both worlds.

Deliverables:
A functional phone app that can read an analyze music files to organize them and play queue and schedule music and play in the background of other applications.
