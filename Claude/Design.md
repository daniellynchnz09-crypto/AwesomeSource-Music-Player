DEFINITIONS:

Music Tracks/Tracks:
what I tend to call an individual music file, as multiple tracks make up an album or EP, also music can be called tracks in the EDM space eg. "Did you drop that track"

EDM:
Stands for Electronic Dance Music, half of my music library is EDM and in this context I use the term as a wide classifer for any music that is electronic in nature, whether that be make by VSTs, sythesizers or any other non authentic instuments.

FEATURES

Library seperation:
If there are two folders that each have a lot of music in them, the user can go into import settings and select if these two folders that the application is sourcing music from should be dedicated separte libraries or not. If they are dedicated separate libraries then the user can choose what library they want access too from the main home page and all the tracks that are not part of that library will be hidden, different libraries can also have different themes set for them and other cutomiziblity features. Liked tracks from one library do not appear in the liked tracks of another library. Or if a playlist has tracks from both libraries in it, then only the tracks from the library selected will show in that playlist, to see all the tracks in the playlist, the user would have to select Library All.

Like:
A user can like a track or album and it it will appear in the liked playlist

Rate:
a user can rate a track or album on a 14 point scale. This rating can be used as a filter in search filters to find the best/worst tracks. The rating scale works as follows: 1 - 4 = Bad, 5 - 6 = Mid, 7 - 9 = Good, 10 = Basically Perfect, 11 = 14 = Above and Beyond.

Search:
A user can search an album, playlist, all songs, albums, artist etc. the search defaults to a text search but you can also apply filters based on other metrics like rating, artist, year, etc.

some buttons:
On every album, playlist, artist page, single (shuffle wont be on singles) there is a selection of buttons which are the play button, shuffle button, add to playlist and add to queue buttons

Audio Visualizer:
The Music player page should have some customizable audio visualizer that isn't just a gif and dymamically reacts to the beat, the perhaps the album art pulses to the beat and the bars are the same colour as the album art and are attached to the sides of the album art. The audio visualizer will also persist on the lock screen of the device and there is an option in settings to turn it off or on and another option to allow it on the borders of the phone ontop of any app like musiks edge.

Queue/up next:
There is a button on the music player to open the queue, where the user can see and change the order for what is on the queue to play next. When a track is added to the queue it is added after every other track that was on the queue before it. If the user holds down on the add to queue button it gives an option to add at the start of the queue or at the end of the queue.

Settings:
the settings button is avalible from the homepage and the player. Some options in the settings inlcude: Play speed (from 0.1x to 5x), Crossfade between tracks (The user can choose the ammount of seconds that the crossfade lasts in seconds), Skip silence between tracks, Control music from lock screen, An option to export and import playlists, options to change the theme (light mode, dark mode), There are also eq controls.

PAGES

Music Player:
the music player is a pop up page that is always visible on all pages of the applications interface except for settings. While navigating other pages the player is minimized to a small bar on the bottom of the screen where the user can play, pause, skip and go back to previous tracks. When the bar is clicked on and the page is open the top half shows the cover art, the background shows a heavily blured and zoomed in version of the cover art, we see detials such as the track name, artist and album as well as controls to play, pause, skip to next, go back to previous and a slider control for the time left in the track. The music player is also avalible to be interacted with from the the notifcations area of the phone with the play pause and duration line, and will continue to play the music while the user is in other apps. If the phone is locked the music will still play and the music player interface will appear on the lockscreen. Playback can also be started from external devices such as a headset with play controls or a speaker etc.

Genres Page:
A page where you can see all the genres of music you have including subgenres: can be ordered by most recently added to or by name etc. when a genre is clicked on you can see all the alblums/tracks that have that genre.

Album Page:
on this page the users can scroll through a album page that lists all of the ablums that they have on their player and they can sort the albums by year, name etc. When a user clicks on an album they are taken to the album page where there is the cover art of the album at the top followed byt the title of the album, it's year and duration (total duration of all tracks in the album), the tracks are then listed in order of track number with their name, track number, duration, and artists. 

Track/Album edit page:
A user can press the 3 dots and open up a menu where they can edit the details/metadata of any track or album to remove or add tracks or add or change detials to existing tracks.

Artist Page: 
On this page the user can scroll through the artists which are sorted by year or quanity or what ever users can set as filters and when they click on an artist by default all their releases are sorted by year but they can be changed based on filters and stuff if the user likes. But the artist page will show all the releases sorted by year by default. There would be headings for each year and each year will list the albums and tracks that they artist released which can be clicked on opened and played.

Tracks:
Just a big list of all the songs/tracks on the device with the album art on the left, title on the right with the artist can be sorted with a varitety of filters and things

Playlists:
A place where the user can see all of the playlists that they have made, there are also a few playlists that are made by default and are automatically updated based on user action, these are Recently added (500 most recently added tracks), Most played (100 most played tracks (ranked with a number system and number of plays noted)), Recently played (100 most recenlty played tracks), Favourite tracks (selected by the user), Awful (rating of 3 or below), Awesome (rating of 10 or above), Roulete (100 tracks, 3 awful tracks for every 7 awesome tracks). The rest are user created playlists that can be ordered and sorted like albums and when clicked on present like albums, a custom image can be set for the thumbnail, otherwise by default it will choose the first track image that has a image. Playlists can also be created from this menu and tracks and albums can be selected to add to playlists.

Organization Page:
Where newly added tracks sit that have not been organazied into the rest of the library or are in the process of being organized sit, the user can watch the organization happen live and can also access the flaged entry pages from here too.

Discovery Page:
This page is used to show the latest releases for artists that the user has music of on their phone. The system would scan what artists they have and their tracks to get a match, then if there are old albums/tracks they don't have or new albums that have been released then a page would be created for that artists that shows everything that the user does not have. the order of the artists on the page is dictated by their latest release, so those with most recent tracks will rise to the top. When the artist is clicked on it will act like the normal artist page where it will list their tracks/albums by year, but the user can't click on any of the singles or tracks inside the albums to listen to them or add them to playlists etc, as it is just a list and not actual songs, as the user would have to get those songs, in which case the entry in discovery with that track or album would dissapear. There's also another page which shows the top 100 recent trending EDM music by edm subgenre if the user wants to find new tracks from new artists.

Cover flow:
There is a seperate navigation method that can be turned on in settings called Cover flow very similar to the old itunes cover flow for ipod, where if a user searches through the album/artist page they will get a menu that shows all cover art of the albums/an artist's releases, and the user can scroll to see all the different covers. When a cover is clicked on the cover flips over and the tracks can be seen and played from there.

Analytics page:
A place where a user can see all of their music analytics similar to a spotfiy wrapped or a soundcloud playback, but a user can look at it any day of the year to watch it update instead of during a specific time. however at a specific time of the year each year it will send an email to the user with graphics on each of these analytics. Also at the same time it will create a playlist with the users 50 most listened to tracks of that year.
